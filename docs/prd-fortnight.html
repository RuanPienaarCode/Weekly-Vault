Obsidian plugin · Product requirements

Fortnight — plan two weeks, live one day

A planning board for Obsidian. You plan the next two weeks on Sunday, then adjust each morning and review each evening. It works over your existing Tasks lines, Nudge reminders and Rhythm practices, and doesn't need a store of its own.

Problem statement

The vault already holds to-dos (Tasks lines scattered across notes), reminders (Nudge), recurring practices and events (Rhythm). But nowhere can you see the next two weeks as a whole and move things between days. Planning happens in your head, or not at all.

Who has it: one person who wants a weekly ritual (Sunday evening or Monday morning, planning two weeks ahead) and a daily one (adjust in the morning, review in the evening), on desktop and iPhone.

The cost of not solving it:

  • Deadlines surface late.
  • Undone to-dos stay silently where they were, or get lost.
  • Weekly practices ("gym 3×/week") never get placed on actual days.
  • Nothing records what happened each day.

Most open to-dos in the first target vault are undated, so a naive "show everything" board would bury you on the first day.

Goals

  1. Sunday session in under 15 minutes: look back, clear Later, and place this week's work on days and the rest into Next week, all in one guided flow.
  2. Nothing slips silently: every unfinished card from a past day is either sorted in the evening review or shown in Today's Slipped group. None disappears and none rolls over automatically.
  3. One source of truth: every to-do the board shows is a Tasks line or a Nudge reminder. Moving a card changes that line, so Tasks queries, Nudge and Rhythm all agree with the board.
  4. Phone-first daily use: "Plan today" and "Review today" each take under 2 minutes on an iPhone, with ≥44px touch targets and no cross-column dragging.
  5. A daily record: each evening review appends a done/moved/dropped summary, plus an optional reflection, to that day's log note.
  6. Weekly practices get placed: weekly-cadence Rhythm practices can be promised to days, and Rhythm's Today view honours those promises.

Non-goals (v1)

Each of these was explicitly deferred during the design interview.

Users & access model

  • Single user, local-first. Everything happens inside the user's own Obsidian vault. There is no login, server, account or network access at runtime.
  • Devices: desktop (the wide board, used for the Sunday session) and iPhone/iPad (a single-day view, used daily). Vault sync, such as iCloud, is the user's own and outside the plugin.
  • Permissions: the plugin reads and writes only:
    • Tasks lines, anywhere in the vault
    • its planner note
    • Rhythm Log and Event notes, by appending or patching frontmatter only
    • its own plugin data
    Nudge reminders are changed only through Nudge's own programming interface.
  • Distribution: a public GitHub repo under AGPL-3.0, aimed at the Obsidian community store. Sample data and defaults are generic.

User stories

The board

  • v1As a planner, I want this week's seven days as columns, a Next week bucket and a Later pile, so that I see my fortnight at a glance.
  • v1As a planner, I want the board to treat the coming Monday as "this week" from Sunday on, so that my Sunday session plans the week ahead.
  • v1As a planner, I want days already gone this week shown as thin columns, so that the rest of the week gets the space.
  • v1As a planner, I want to expand Next week into its seven days, so that I can pin something with a real date without planning the whole week in detail.
  • v1As a planner, I want to drag a to-do between days, Next week and Later, so that rescheduling is one gesture.
  • v1As a planner, I want each day to show events first, by time, and then to-dos in the order I dragged them, so that the day reads as it will happen.
  • v1As a planner, I want a to-do with a deadline (📅) but no planned day to show on its due day with a deadline badge, so that deadlines can't hide.
  • v1As a planner, I want completed cards dimmed in a collapsed group at the bottom of their day, so that I can see what I got through without clutter.
  • v1As a planner, I want unhandled cards from past days shown in a Slipped group at the top of Today, so that nothing slips silently.
  • v1As a planner, I want each card to show where it lives (which note, or a Nudge bell), so that I can open it in its note.

Cards

  • v1As a planner, I want a quick-add field on every column with a To-do/Event switch, so that I can capture something straight into the right day.
  • v1As a planner, I want "14:00 dentist" to become an event at 14:00, so that typing a time is enough.
  • v1As a planner, I want new to-dos saved as Tasks lines in my planner note, so that Tasks queries find them.
  • v1As a planner, I want new events saved as Rhythm Event notes, so that Rhythm and Vista see them too.
  • v1As a planner, I want events shown locked (they can't be dragged), so that I don't move a real appointment by accident.
  • v1As a planner, I want ticking a repeating to-do to create its next occurrence exactly as Tasks would, so that the planner and Tasks never disagree.

Sources

  • v1As a planner, I want every dated open Tasks line in the vault on the board, so that I don't have to move my to-dos anywhere.
  • v1As a planner, I want undated to-dos in Later only from my planner note and folders I choose, so that Later is a short list of real candidates, not my whole vault.
  • v1As a planner, I want to exclude folders, so that templates and archives stay off the board.
  • v1As a planner, I want my Nudge reminders shown as cards, and moving one to change its date through Nudge, so that Nudge keeps reminding me on the right day.
  • v1As a planner, I want Rhythm Event notes shown as events on their day.
  • laterAs a planner, I want to subscribe to an iCloud or Google calendar feed, shown read-only, so that my appointments appear without retyping.

Rhythm practices

  • v1As a planner, I want a Practices owed tray of my weekly-cadence Rhythm practices, showing how many are still unplaced, so that I can tell when the week is fully spread.
  • v1As a planner, I want dragging a practice onto a day to record it as promised to that day in Rhythm's log, so that Rhythm's Today view agrees with the board.
  • v1As a planner, I want ticking a practice card to record it as done in Rhythm's log.
  • v1As a planner, I want daily practices shown only as a small count per day ("4 daily"), so that they don't flood every column.

Phone day view

  • v1As a phone user, I want a one-day view with a day switcher, so that the board is usable on a 375px screen.
  • v1As a phone user, I want tapping a card to open a sheet with Today · Tomorrow · Pick a day · Next week · Later · Done · Drop, so that every move is one tap away without dragging.
  • v1As a phone user, I want to long-press and drag to reorder cards within the day.

Routines

  • v1As a planner, I want a guided Plan the fortnight flow (look back → clear Later → place), with Skip on each step, so that the Sunday ritual has a shape.
  • v1As a planner, I want a Plan today mode in the morning that shows today, Slipped and the most urgent Later items, so that I can pull things in and push things out.
  • v1As a planner, I want a Review today mode in the evening that takes each unfinished card in turn and asks tomorrow / pick a day / Later / drop, so that each one gets a deliberate decision.
  • v1As a planner, I want the review to append a ## Review section (done / moved / dropped, plus an optional one-line reflection) to today's log note, so that I have a record of each day.
  • v1As a planner, I want to choose which folder's day note receives the review (default: Rhythm's Log), so that it fits how I journal.

Testing decisions

Seam 1 — planBoard() (pure)

Input: { today, tasks, reminders, practices, log, events, settings }. Output: the board (columns → ordered cards, Slipped, Practices owed, daily counts). Most of the product's rules are tested here with in-memory fixtures:

  • the Sunday jump
  • placement from ⏳, 🛫, 📅 or no date
  • which undated to-dos are included
  • ordering by 🆔 and priority
  • done and slipped grouping
  • practice cadence

Seam 2 — store over a stand-in Obsidian app

The stand-in fakes the vault, Tasks' apiV1 and Nudge's store. Each test runs an action — move, tick, reorder, add, writeReview — and asserts:

  • the resulting file text, for example that the line gained ⏳ and kept 📅, or that the Log body was untouched and ## Review appended
  • the calls to the other plugins, for example that a Nudge move went through setDue and a tick went through Tasks

Guards: ios-hazards.test.cjs (copied from the sibling repos) and a "never rewrites a note body" check.

Not unit-tested: the screens (board, phone view, routines). They are checked in the _preview harness on port 8824, then in the real vault.

What a good test looks like here: it exercises behaviour through planBoard() or store only, and asserts board output, file text or calls to the other plugins. It never reaches into internal helpers.

Prior art to mirror

  • rhythm-vault/tests/model.test.cjs: planDay() over fixture weeks. This is the shape for Seam 1.
  • nudge-vault/tests/store.test.cjs with _stub.cjs: the store over a stand-in vault. This is the shape for Seam 2.
  • nudge-vault/tests/tasks.test.cjs: parsing and serialising Tasks lines.
  • */tests/ios-hazards.test.cjs: copy it unchanged.
  • Test runner: scripts/run-tests.mjs, plain Node, no framework.

Where the critical-path coverage sits

  • Tasks line parse and serialise, round-tripping unknown emoji and text byte-for-byte
  • Placement and week-window rules (Seam 1)
  • Moves and ticks, and how each is routed to Tasks, Nudge or Rhythm (Seam 2)
  • Review writer (Seam 2)

Data model sketch

Fortnight stores almost nothing of its own. It is a view over other plugins' data plus a thin layer of ordering.

Card (in memory, built by planBoard)
 ├─ kind: todo | event | practice
 ├─ source ──► TasksLine   (file path, line no., original text)         1 card : 1 line
 │           ► NudgeItem   (Nudge store id)                             1 card : 1 reminder
 │           ► RhythmEvent (Rhythm/Events/*.md  — date, time)           1 card : 1 note
 │           ► Practice    (Rhythm/Practices/*.md — cadence, days)      1 practice : 0..n cards/week
 ├─ slot: day(YYYY-MM-DD) | nextWeek | later          ← derived from ⏳ / 🛫 / none
 ├─ due?: YYYY-MM-DD                                   ← 📅, never changed by moves
 └─ order?: number                                     ← from plugin data by 🆔

TasksLine   "- [ ] text ⏳ 2026-09-30 📅 2026-10-02 🆔 f3k9"
RhythmLog   Rhythm/Log/YYYY-MM-DD.md  frontmatter plan[] / done[]  + appended "## Review"
Plugin data { order: { "<🆔>": n }, settings: { plannerNote, includeFolders[], excludeFolders[],
              reviewFolder, weekStart: 1, calendarUrls: [] } }

Slots: day → ⏳ · nextWeek → 🛫 = next Monday and no ⏳ · later → neither.

Success metrics

  • Ritual kept: in the four weeks after v1 ships, the Sunday flow is completed at least 3 times out of 4.
  • Daily review kept: at least 5 ## Review sections a week in Rhythm/Log over the same four weeks.
  • Nothing silent: zero open to-dos with ⏳ more than 7 days in the past at any Sunday session.
  • Timing: the Sunday flow takes ≤15 min and each daily mode ≤2 min, self-timed on the first three uses.
  • Agreement: in each phase's click-through, the Tasks query, Nudge and Rhythm Today views show the same days as the board.
  • Safety: no note body is ever rewritten. The guard test is green on every build, and no data-loss report appears in the first month.

Open questions

Not settled in the design interview. Each has a recommended default that stands unless changed.

#QuestionRecommended default
1What does Drop do to a Tasks line?Mark it cancelled (- [-], which Tasks treats as cancelled), rather than deleting it. The text stays in its note and the action can be undone.
2A Nudge reminder with a ⏰ time: to-do or event?A to-do that shows its time, and can be moved. Moving it keeps the ⏰ time and changes the date through setDue.
3Nudge cards moved to Next week or Later: Nudge has no 🛫, and removing the due date stops reminders.Next week sets the due date to next Monday. Later asks for confirmation ("stop reminding?") before clearing the due date.
4Does the evening review also sort unfinished practices?No. Rhythm already tracks what's owed; unticked practices just drop back into the tray.
5What exactly does Plan today show beyond Today and Slipped?Today, Slipped, today's due deadlines, and the top 5 Later items (planner note first). A "pull in" button on each.
6Can events be edited from the board?Tap opens a sheet to change the date or time for planner-created (Rhythm) events. Calendar-feed events, once they exist, will be read-only.
7Does the Sunday look-back write anything?Yes: a short ## Fortnight plan block appended to that Sunday's log note (what slipped, and how many cards were placed).
